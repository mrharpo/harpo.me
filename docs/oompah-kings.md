# **The OomPah Kings**

**Authentic German music for Oktoberfest, or any occasion!**

The **OomPah Kings** are ready to *Roll Out The Barrel* for your next event! For parties, Oktoberfests, or any occasion where live music is appreciated!

Featuring authentic German music including polkas, waltzes, schottisches, mazurkas, and many traditional regional songs. But it’s more than just traditional German music, our setlist also includes Italian, French, Polish, Sweedish, and even American songs!
